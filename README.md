# GEOG5003

Data held here supports a project exploring the relationship between population demographics and flood warning prepardness. Environment Agency data is imported to show Flood Warning polygons, joined with the number of properties signed up for warnings. This is later filtered to explore the warning areas in Nottighamshire.

LSOAs for Nottingham City, with Indices of Multiple Deprivation (2025) data, are subsequently  joined to explore the relationship between flood warning signup rate and deprivation. This is designed to support Nottingham City Council and Enviornment Agency staff to identify where more engagement may be needed to ensure vulnerable communities are sufficiently warned about flooding. A low sign up rate can be indicative of low awareness of living in an area at risk of flood and more engagement should be sought to ensure safe action during a flood event.
